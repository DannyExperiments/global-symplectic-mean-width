# Claim scope and limitations

The theorem applies in every dimension 2n, n ≥ 1, to compact convex K with nonempty interior and JK = K, with J(x,y) = (−y,x). The acting maps P are real linear symplectic maps for the standard J. The normalization is twice the average of the support function over the unit sphere. Width is minimized at K, and the equality maps are exactly the orthogonal symplectic maps.

Toric symmetry implies JK = K. The Lagrangian bidisk also satisfies this condition. The theorem supplies a sufficient symmetry condition for global linear minimality. It gives no classification of all linear minimizers. The nonlinear first-variation problem for the bidisk and the global nonlinear optimization problem of the follow-up lie outside the theorem.

Green’s characterization covers the planar inequality. The higher-dimensional literature comparison is bounded. The exact replay checks finite examples. The analytical proof establishes the general result. Internally reviewed, not peer reviewed. No proof-assistant formalization is included.
