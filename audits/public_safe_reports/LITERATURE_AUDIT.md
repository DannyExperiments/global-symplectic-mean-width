# Q1223 source and literature audit

## Exact statement

The pinned catalog at commit `ca8d4d44ba3db527f19f28928c240168f545ecfe` asks whether a toric convex body minimizes mean width over the linear symplectic group. Ahn and Kerman state the same infimum in Conjecture 1.6. They use normalized spherical measure in the integral of the sum of opposing support functions, which equals twice the integral of a single support function. The proof applies to compact convex bodies with nonempty interior and invariance under the standard orthogonal complex structure (J). Every toric body has that invariance. [Corrected source, §1, Conjecture 1.6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.02870v3#page=2)

## Closest results

- Ahn and Kerman's v2 Theorem 1.18 asserted the same global toric inequality via symplectic polyannuli. Theorem 1.19 asserted a global statement for the Lagrangian bidisk. In v3, Remark 1.7 identifies an error in the earlier toric proof and states that the conjecture remains open even for symplectic ellipsoids and polydisks in dimension four. Theorem 1.4 gives a local toric result. Proposition 1.12 and its proof in §5.1 establish the local minimum for the Lagrangian bidisk, strictly away from the identity in the positive symplectic factor. Proposition 1.12 also establishes that the bidisk lies outside unitary images of toric bodies. The recovered argument supplies the global linear toric theorem and global linear bidisk corollary directly. [Earlier v2 claims, §§1.4, 3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.02870v2) · [Corrected v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.02870v3)
- Green's Theorem 1 characterizes planar perimeter minimizers under area-preserving affine transformations by vanishing second Fourier coefficients of the support function. Planar mean width is perimeter divided by pi and `Sp(2,R)=SL(2,R)`. A quarter-turn leaves a J-invariant support function invariant and negates both second harmonics, so Green already gives the planar inequality. [Green (1953), §§2–3](https://msp.org/pjm/1953/3-2/pjm-v3-n2-p08-p.pdf) · [Ahn–Kerman follow-up, Theorem 3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.07220#S3)
- Giannopoulos and Milman characterize minimum mean-width position under the full special linear group by the scalar second-moment condition for the spherical support-function weight. Quarter-turn symmetry in higher dimension does not imply that condition. In four dimensions a product of planar disks of radii 1 and 4 has mean width 20/3. Scaling the complex planes by 2 and 1/2 has real determinant one and yields radii 2 and 2 with width 16/3. The scaling does not preserve the symplectic form. The formula is obtained by integrating the two plane lengths, each of whose squared lengths is uniform on [0,1]. [Giannopoulos–Milman, §3, Theorem 3.1](https://www.esi.ac.at/preprints/esi688.pdf#page=8)
- The authors' follow-up separates linear and nonlinear variational problems. Its Proposition 3.7 concerns local linear minimality and its Conjecture 3.8 concerns a nonlinear infimum. The corrected original paper's Question 1.13 asks about a nonlinear first derivative for the bidisk. Proposition 1.14 uses Ramos's work to give a global nonlinear decrease for that bidisk. The linear theorem addresses Conjecture 1.6 and yields a separate bidisk corollary. [Ahn–Kerman follow-up, §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.07220#S3) · [Corrected v3, Proposition 1.12 through Proposition 1.14](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.02870v3#page=4)

## Finding

A bounded primary-literature search checked the exact conjecture, corrected revision and prior claims, both authors' title and follow-up, planar and full special-linear minimization, toric and quarter-turn symmetries, and the Gaussian support-function/polar route. The search found no valid prior theorem giving the general multidimensional linear statement with its equality class. The source match is exact. Literature coverage is bounded. The proof uses standard symplectic polar decomposition, Gaussian covariance comparison and convexity.

The pinned catalog also links Ahn's 2025 thesis. Its stable institutional record predates the 2026 correction. Search-index excerpts were available, but a stable full-text endpoint was unavailable during this pass. The authors' own corrected version explicitly supersedes their earlier global toric proof.
